Account Deletion Policy

Moko TV is an entertainment application that provides AI-generated short drama videos and digital content. We respect user privacy and provide users with the ability to request deletion of their account and associated data.

1. What Data Will Be Deleted?

When an account deletion request is approved, the following information may be permanently removed from our systems:

  • Name (if provided)
  • Email address
  • Account profile information
  • User preferences and settings
  • Watch history associated with the account
  • Saved content and app activity data
  • Any other personal information linked to the account

Deleted data may not be recoverable.

2. How Can Users Delete Their Account?

A. Inside the App

Users may delete their account directly from the application:

Profile → Settings → Delete Account

Once confirmed, the account deletion request will be processed and associated data will be removed according to this policy.

B. By Email Request

Users may also request account deletion by contacting us via email:

Email: mokotv00@gmail.com
Subject: Delete My Moko TV Account

The request should be sent from the registered email address or include the registered email associated with the account.

We aim to process valid deletion requests within 7 business days.

3. What Happens After Account Deletion?

  • Access to the account will be permanently disabled.
  • Associated personal information will be removed from active systems.
  • Saved preferences, watch history, and account-related data may be deleted.
  • A new account may be created in the future using a valid email address.

4. Retained Data

Certain information may be retained for a limited period where required by law, security needs, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or regulatory compliance.

  • Legal compliance records
  • Security and fraud prevention logs
  • Required audit records

5. Google Play Subscriptions

Deleting your Moko TV account does not automatically cancel any active Google Play subscription.

Subscriptions must be cancelled separately through your Google Play account.

6. Policy Updates

Moko TV may update this Account Deletion Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted on this page.
